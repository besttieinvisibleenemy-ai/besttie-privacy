Besttie Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Besttie ("we") is an Android app for chatting with an AI companion about books. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your choices. Contact: besttieinvisibleenemy@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Google account (optional): if you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL and Google account ID. We use them only to create your Besttie account and keep your books and chats across devices. Guest mode needs no account.
  • Anonymous app ID: a random identifier generated on your device so the server can keep your daily session in guest mode.
  • Chats: the book you pick each day, the questions you send and the replies.
  • Uploaded books: files you choose to upload (.txt, .epub, .pdf). We store the extracted text, text passages and their numeric search embeddings so the AI can quote from them. Uploads are private to your account or device.
  • Settings such as reply language, stored on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID.

2. Voice

If you tap the microphone, speech is converted to text by your phone's speech-recognition service (on-device where supported). Audio is never sent to Besttie's servers. Only the text you review and send is. Read-aloud uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. The microphone permission is requested only when you first tap the mic.

3. How data is processed and shared

  • Google Gemini API: to generate replies, your question, recent conversation and relevant book passages are sent to Google's Gemini API. Uploaded book text is sent there to create search embeddings. Google processes this under the Gemini API Terms. While Besttie uses Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Do not send personal or sensitive information in chats or uploads.
  • Render: our server and database are hosted by Render (render.com), which stores account data, chats and uploads on our behalf.
  • Google Sign-In is provided by Google via Android Credential Manager.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share data with advertisers or data brokers. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

4. Retention and deletion

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, uploaded books, their index and your chats from our server immediately.
  • Delete on an uploaded book removes it and its chats.
  • Settings → Delete all my data on this device clears local data.
  • You can also request deletion by emailing besttieinvisibleenemy@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.
  • Guest data that is never deleted is kept while the service runs. Server logs do not contain chat text.

5. Children

Besttie is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

6. AI disclaimer

Replies are AI-generated, may contain mistakes, and are not the views of a book's author.

7. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
